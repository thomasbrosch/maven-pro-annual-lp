Pro Annual: $239 for your first year, $160 off the regular $399. Pro Monthly: $37 a month for your first 3 months, then $49. Pro Lifetime: $999 once, $700 off the regular $1,699.

Which plan should I choose?

All three plans include the same Maven Pro. Annual costs the least for your first year. Lifetime suits you if you plan to use Pro for three years or more. Monthly suits you if you only need a few months.

Who can get this price?

$239 is Maven’s Black Friday price for your first year of Pro Annual. It isn’t available to existing annual subscribers.

Why choose Maven over other learning platforms?

Maven combines expert instruction, practical projects and skills assessments to help you build data and AI skills, and demonstrate what you’ve learned. Benchmark and final assessments in eligible courses measure your progress, while projects and credentials help you showcase your work. For teams, SkillSight gives leaders visibility into skills and learning progress.

What’s included in Free versus Pro?

Free never expires and doesn’t need a credit card. It includes a personal learning plan, sample lessons, practice tool assessments, your project portfolio and public profile, and the Data Playground. Pro adds all courses, paths and projects, live workshops with expert instructors, benchmark and final assessments, badges and credentials, and course discussions. Compare Free and Pro.

Do subscriptions renew automatically?

Monthly and yearly Pro subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel, and we email yearly subscribers before their renewal date. Lifetime is a one-time payment and doesn’t renew. See how to cancel.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. You can upgrade from Free to Pro at any time, or contact us about moving your team to Enterprise or Enterprise + Live. Personal and team subscriptions are separate: joining a team doesn’t automatically cancel or transfer your personal Pro subscription. See how to change your plan.

What’s your refund policy?

Monthly and yearly Pro payments, including renewals, and Lifetime purchases are non-refundable unless required by applicable law. You can cancel a monthly or yearly Pro subscription to stop its next renewal and keep access through the period you’ve paid for. See our refund policy for full details.
